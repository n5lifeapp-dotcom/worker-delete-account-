N5 Life Worker – Delete Account

If you are an N5 Life Worker and want to delete your account, you can request account deletion by contacting us.

Important: Account deletion may permanently remove your worker profile, account information, booking information, and other data associated with your account, subject to applicable legal and business requirements.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email from your registered email address.
  2. Use the subject: Delete My N5 Life Worker Account
  3. Include your registered phone number.
  4. Our team will verify your request and process the deletion.

Delete Account Request

Email: n5lifeapp@gmail.com

What Happens After the Request?

We will verify the account ownership before processing the request. After verification, the account and eligible associated information will be deleted or deactivated according to our data retention requirements.

Certain information may be retained when required by law, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate business requirements.
